# Brazilian-Style Moscow Mule (Moscow Mule Brasileiro)

**Category:** Cocktails & Beverages  
**Cuisine:** Brazilian-Fusion  
**Servings:** 1 cocktail  
**Prep Time:** 30 minutes (plus 30 minutes for syrup)  
**Total Time:** 1 hour

## Description

This Instagram-famous Brazilian variation of the Moscow Mule was created by São Paulo bartender Marcelo Serrano around 2012. It features three stunning layers: a homemade ginger syrup base, the classic vodka-lime-ginger beer cocktail middle, and a sweet ginger emulsion foam on top. Now more popular than the original in Brazil, this cocktail showcases molecular gastronomy techniques and fresh Brazilian ingredients.

## Ingredients

### For the Homemade Ginger Syrup (makes 1 cup)
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup demerara sugar (or regular granulated sugar)
- 3-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and sliced
- Optional: 2 tablespoons honey

### For the Cocktail (per drink)
- 2 oz vodka (or cachaça for authentic Brazilian version)
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 4 oz ginger beer
- 1 oz homemade ginger syrup
- Crushed ice

### For the Sweet Ginger Emulsion (makes 4-6 servings)
- 2 oz fresh ginger juice (from about 4 oz fresh ginger)
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 1 oz simple syrup
- 1 egg white (pasteurized recommended)
- 2 oz citrus soda (Sprite or 7-Up)
- 1/4 teaspoon xanthan gum (or 1/2 teaspoon lecithin)

### For Garnish
- Fresh lime wedges or wheels
- Fresh mint sprigs
- Candied ginger (optional)

## Instructions

### Make the Homemade Ginger Syrup (prepare ahead)
1. Combine water and demerara sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add sliced ginger and bring to a boil, stirring until sugar dissolves completely.
3. Reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes until flavors infuse.
4. Remove from heat, stir in honey if using, and let cool completely.
5. Strain out ginger pieces and store syrup in a clean bottle. Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

### Make the Sweet Ginger Emulsion
**Method 1 - With iSi Siphon (recommended):**
1. Peel and grate fresh ginger, then squeeze through cheesecloth to extract 2 oz juice.
2. Combine ginger juice, lime juice, simple syrup, egg white, citrus soda, and xanthan gum in the iSi siphon canister.
3. Shake vigorously for 30 seconds to combine.
4. Charge with 2 CO2 cartridges according to manufacturer instructions.
5. Shake again for 15 seconds. Refrigerate for at least 15 minutes before using.

**Method 2 - Without Siphon (dry shake method):**
1. Extract ginger juice as above.
2. Combine all ingredients except citrus soda in a cocktail shaker.
3. Dry shake (without ice) vigorously for 45-60 seconds until very frothy.
4. Add citrus soda gently and shake lightly for 5 seconds.
5. Use immediately while foam is stable.

### Assemble the Cocktail
1. Fill a copper mug or highball glass with crushed ice to the top.
2. Pour 1 oz homemade ginger syrup directly into the bottom of the glass.
3. In a separate mixing glass, combine vodka (or cachaça) and fresh lime juice with ice. Stir briefly.
4. Using a bar spoon held against the inside of the glass, slowly pour the vodka-lime mixture over the back of the spoon onto the ice to create a layer above the syrup.
5. Gently pour ginger beer over the back of the spoon in the same manner.
6. Do not stir - the layers should remain distinct.
7. If using siphon: Hold upside down and dispense a generous layer of ginger foam on top.
   If using dry shake method: Spoon the foam gently onto the top of the drink.
8. Garnish with lime wedge, fresh mint sprig, and candied ginger if desired.
9. Serve immediately with a straw, instructing guests to stir before drinking to combine all three layers.

## Chef's Notes

- **Cachaça substitution:** For an authentic Brazilian experience, replace vodka with cachaça (Brazilian sugarcane spirit).
- **Foam stability:** The xanthan gum or lecithin helps stabilize the foam. Without it, the foam will dissipate within 5-10 minutes.
- **Layering technique:** Success depends on density differences - the syrup is heaviest, the cocktail middle, and foam lightest. Pour slowly and gently.
- **Ginger beer choice:** Use a spicy, high-quality ginger beer for best results. Brazilian bartenders often prefer Fever-Tree or locally made versions.
- **Make ahead:** Syrup can be made 1-2 weeks ahead. Foam mixture (in siphon) can be made 2-3 days ahead and kept refrigerated.
- **Scaling:** For parties, prepare large batches of syrup and foam mixture, assembling individual drinks as needed.
