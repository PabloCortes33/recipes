# Chocolate Brownies

A rich and fudgy chocolate brownie recipe that's perfect for dessert or an afternoon treat.

## Ingredients

- 200g dark chocolate, chopped
- 175g unsalted butter
- 3 large eggs
- 250g caster sugar
- 75g plain flour
- 50g cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 100g chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a 20cm square baking tin with parchment paper.

2. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water, stirring until smooth. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs and sugar until pale and fluffy, about 3-4 minutes.

4. Pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture and fold gently until combined.

5. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Fold until just combined - don't overmix.

6. Stir in the vanilla extract and chocolate chips if using.

7. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and smooth the top.

8. Bake for 25-30 minutes. The brownies should have a slight crust on top but still be soft in the center.

9. Let cool completely in the tin before cutting into squares.

## Notes

- For extra fudgy brownies, underbake slightly and refrigerate before cutting.
- Store in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
- Can be frozen for up to 3 months.

## Servings

Makes 16 brownies

## Prep Time

15 minutes

## Cook Time

30 minutes
