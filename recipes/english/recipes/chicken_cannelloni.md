# Chicken Cannelloni

Rich pasta tubes filled with tender chicken and creamy béchamel sauce, baked until golden and bubbling. This classic Italian comfort dish is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

**Yields:** 4-6 servings  
**Prep Time:** 30 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Filling
- 500g (1 lb) chicken breast, cooked and shredded
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 200g (7 oz) ricotta cheese
- 100g (3.5 oz) mozzarella cheese, grated
- 50g (1.8 oz) Parmesan cheese, grated
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil or parsley, chopped
- Pinch of nutmeg

### For the Pasta
- 12-14 cannelloni tubes (or fresh lasagna sheets)

### For the Béchamel Sauce
- 60g (4 tablespoons) butter
- 60g (1/2 cup) all-purpose flour
- 1 litre (4 cups) whole milk, warmed
- Salt, pepper, and nutmeg to taste

### For the Topping
- 400g (14 oz) tomato sauce or passata
- 100g (3.5 oz) mozzarella cheese, grated
- 50g (1.8 oz) Parmesan cheese, grated
- Fresh basil leaves

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the filling:** Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, sautéed onion and garlic, ricotta, mozzarella, Parmesan, egg, herbs, nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Mix well and set aside.

2. **Make the béchamel sauce:** Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add flour and whisk continuously for 2 minutes to create a roux. Gradually add the warm milk, whisking constantly to avoid lumps. Cook until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon, about 8-10 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg.

3. **Prepare the baking dish:** Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce on the bottom of a large baking dish (approximately 30x20cm).

4. **Fill the cannelloni:** If using dried tubes, fill them with the chicken mixture using a piping bag or small spoon. If using lasagna sheets, place filling along one edge and roll up. Arrange the filled cannelloni in a single layer in the prepared dish.

5. **Add the sauces:** Pour the remaining tomato sauce over the cannelloni, then cover completely with the béchamel sauce. Sprinkle the grated mozzarella and Parmesan on top.

6. **Bake:** Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

7. **Serve:** Let rest for 5-10 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil leaves. Serve hot with a green salad on the side.
