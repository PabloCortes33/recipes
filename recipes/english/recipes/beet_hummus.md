# Beet Hummus (Hummus de Betarraga)

A vibrant, modern fusion dish popular in Chile, combining earthy roasted beets with creamy chickpea hummus. This stunning pink dip offers a perfect balance of sweet, tangy, and nutty flavors, making it ideal for celebrations, cheese boards, and everyday snacking.

**Yields:** 6-8 servings (about 2.5 cups)  
**Prep time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook time:** 60 minutes  
**Total time:** 75 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Roasted Beets
- 2 medium beets (about 1 pound)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Aluminum foil for wrapping

### For the Hummus
- 1.5 cups cooked chickpeas (or 1 can, 15 oz, drained)
- 3 tablespoons tahini
- 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
- 2 tablespoons aquafaba (reserved chickpea liquid)
- 2 ice cubes
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus more for serving

### For Garnish
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Fresh herbs (parsley or cilantro)
- Za'atar or feta crumbles (optional)

## Instructions

### Roast the Beets
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Scrub beets clean, trim ends, and rub with olive oil.
3. Wrap each beet individually in aluminum foil.
4. Place on a baking sheet and roast for 50-60 minutes, until tender when pierced with a fork.
5. Remove from oven, let cool slightly, then peel the skins off (they should slip off easily). Cut into quarters.

### Make the Hummus
6. In a food processor, combine tahini and lemon juice. Blend for 30 seconds until smooth and creamy.
7. Add the roasted beet quarters, chickpeas, garlic, cumin, coriander, and salt. Pulse to combine.
8. With the processor running, add aquafaba and ice cubes. Blend for 2-3 minutes.
9. Drizzle in the olive oil while continuing to blend. Process for another 2-3 minutes until the hummus is very smooth, light, and fluffy.
10. Taste and adjust seasoning with more salt or lemon juice if needed.

### Serve
11. Transfer to a serving bowl, creating a well in the center with the back of a spoon.
12. Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil and garnish with sesame seeds, fresh herbs, and za'atar or feta if desired.
13. Serve with fresh vegetables (carrots, celery, cucumber, bell peppers), pita chips, crackers, or bread.

## Notes

- **For silkiest texture:** Remove chickpea skins before blending (optional but effective).
- **Color tip:** Always use fresh roasted beets for the most vibrant red color. Pre-packaged beets will produce a muted pink.
- **Faster method:** Boil peeled beets for 20-40 minutes instead of roasting, though this produces less intense flavor and color.
- **Storage:** Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for 4-5 days. Add a thin layer of olive oil on top to preserve freshness.
- **Variations:** This hummus works beautifully as a sandwich spread, toast topping, or grain bowl base.
