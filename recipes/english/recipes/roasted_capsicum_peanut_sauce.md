# Roasted Capsicum and Peanut Dipping Sauce

A vibrant, flavorful dipping sauce combining sweet roasted red peppers with rich peanuts. Perfect for vegetables, grilled meats, spring rolls, or as a spread for sandwiches and wraps.

**Yields:** About 1 1/2 cups
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
*   2 large red bell peppers (capsicums), about 1 pound (450g)
*   3 cloves garlic, unpeeled
*   1/2 cup roasted unsalted peanuts (75g)
*   2 tablespoons peanut butter (smooth or crunchy)
*   2 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari
*   1 tablespoon rice vinegar or lime juice
*   1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
*   1 teaspoon sesame oil
*   1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)
*   2-4 tablespoons water, as needed for consistency
*   Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

**Equipment:**
*   Baking sheet
*   Aluminum foil
*   Food processor or blender
*   Small bowl

**Instructions:**

1.  **Roast the Peppers:** Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C). Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil. Place the whole red peppers and unpeeled garlic cloves on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, turning the peppers every 8-10 minutes, until the pepper skins are blackened and blistered on all sides. The garlic should be soft and golden.
2.  **Steam and Peel:** Remove from oven and immediately transfer the peppers to a bowl. Cover tightly with plastic wrap or a plate and let steam for 10 minutes. This will make the skins easier to remove. Once cooled enough to handle, peel off the charred skins, remove the stems and seeds. Peel the roasted garlic cloves.
3.  **Blend the Sauce:** In a food processor or blender, combine the roasted peppers, roasted garlic, peanuts, peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, and red pepper flakes (if using). Pulse until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed. Add water 1 tablespoon at a time until you reach your desired consistency (thicker for a dip, thinner for a sauce).
4.  **Season and Serve:** Taste and adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, or additional soy sauce if needed. Transfer to a serving bowl. The sauce can be served immediately or refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 1 week. Bring to room temperature before serving for best flavor.
