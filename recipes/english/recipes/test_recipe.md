# Classic Grilled Cheese Sandwich

A simple and delicious test recipe for a classic grilled cheese sandwich with perfectly melted cheese and golden, crispy bread.

**Yields:** 1 sandwich
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Cook time:** 5 minutes

**Ingredients:**
*   2 slices of bread (white, sourdough, or your preference)
*   2 tablespoons butter, softened
*   2-3 slices of cheddar cheese (or cheese of your choice)
*   Pinch of salt (optional)

**Equipment:**
*   Non-stick skillet or griddle
*   Spatula

**Instructions:**

1.  **Butter the Bread:** Spread softened butter evenly on one side of each bread slice.
2.  **Heat the Pan:** Place a non-stick skillet over medium heat and let it warm up for about 1 minute.
3.  **Assemble the Sandwich:** Place one slice of bread butter-side down in the skillet. Add the cheese slices on top. Place the second bread slice on top with the butter-side facing up.
4.  **Cook First Side:** Cook for 2-3 minutes until the bottom is golden brown. Check by gently lifting the edge with a spatula.
5.  **Flip and Finish:** Carefully flip the sandwich and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the second side is golden brown and the cheese is fully melted.
6.  **Serve:** Remove from heat, let cool for 30 seconds, cut in half if desired, and serve immediately.
