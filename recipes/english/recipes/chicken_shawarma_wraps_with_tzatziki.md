# Chicken Shawarma Wraps with Tzatziki

A flavorful Middle Eastern dish featuring marinated chicken thighs roasted with garlic, served with homemade tzatziki sauce and fresh salad in warm pita bread.

**Yields:** 4-6 servings  
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus marinating time)  
**Cook Time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken Shawarma
- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- ¼ cup Greek yogurt
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 heads garlic (for roasting)
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1½ tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne

### For the Tzatziki Sauce
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- ½ cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp dill
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper

### For the Salad Filling
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, quartered
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- ½ cup cucumber, diced
- ¼ cup parsley, chopped
- ½ tsp dill
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper

### For Assembly
- Pita bread or naan

## Instructions

### Prepare the Chicken Shawarma
1. Slice chicken thighs into thin strips and add to a large bowl.
2. Add olive oil, tomato paste, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, and all the spices. Mix until evenly coated.
3. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to overnight in the refrigerator.
4. Cut the tops off two garlic heads, drizzle with olive oil and salt, wrap in foil, and place in a baking dish with the chicken.
5. Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes until the chicken is golden and slightly charred.
6. Squeeze the roasted garlic into the chicken and toss before serving.

### Make the Tzatziki Sauce
1. Mix Greek yogurt, grated cucumber, mayonnaise, olive oil, lemon juice, dill, salt, and pepper in a bowl until smooth.
2. Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.

### Prepare the Salad Filling
1. Toss cherry tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, parsley, dill, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper together.
2. Let sit for 10 minutes in the refrigerator so the flavors come together.

### Assemble the Wraps
1. Warm pita or naan bread.
2. Spread tzatziki sauce on the bread.
3. Add sliced chicken shawarma.
4. Spoon salad filling over the chicken.
5. Drizzle with additional tzatziki sauce and wrap.
