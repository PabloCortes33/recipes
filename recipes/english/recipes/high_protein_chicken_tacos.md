# High-Protein Chicken Tacos

A quick and flavorful high-protein taco recipe featuring seasoned chicken breast, black beans, and Greek yogurt. Perfect for a healthy weeknight dinner ready in under 30 minutes.

**Yields:** 4 servings (3 tacos each)  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken
- 500g (1 lb) chicken breast, diced into small cubes
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For Assembly
- 12 small corn or whole wheat tortillas
- 1 can (400g) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 200g plain Greek yogurt
- 100g reduced-fat cheese, shredded
- 1 cup lettuce, shredded
- 1 large tomato, diced
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- Lime wedges for serving
- Hot sauce (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Season the chicken:** In a bowl, combine the diced chicken with cumin, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat evenly.

2. **Cook the chicken:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned chicken and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through (internal temperature 75°C/165°F).

3. **Warm the beans:** While the chicken cooks, heat the black beans in a small saucepan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes until warmed through. Season with a pinch of salt.

4. **Warm the tortillas:** Heat tortillas in a dry skillet for 20-30 seconds per side, or wrap in damp paper towels and microwave for 30 seconds.

5. **Assemble the tacos:** Place tortillas on plates. Layer each with chicken, black beans, shredded cheese, lettuce, tomatoes, a dollop of Greek yogurt, and fresh cilantro.

6. **Serve:** Serve immediately with lime wedges and hot sauce on the side.

## Nutritional Information (per serving - 3 tacos)
- Calories: ~520
- Protein: ~45g
- Carbohydrates: ~48g
- Fat: ~15g

## Notes
- For extra protein, add a fried egg on top of each taco
- Substitute chicken with lean ground turkey or beef for variety
- Greek yogurt provides protein while serving as a healthy sour cream alternative
- Make it meal prep friendly: cook chicken and beans ahead, store separately, and assemble when ready to eat
