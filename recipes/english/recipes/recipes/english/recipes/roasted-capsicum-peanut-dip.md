# Roasted Capsicum and Peanut Dip

A creamy, smoky, and savory dip with a nutty finish. Perfect for an appetizer with fresh vegetables, crackers, or as a spread on sandwiches.

**Yields:** 1.5 cups  
**Prep time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients:

- Main:
  - 2 large red capsicums (bell peppers)
  - 1/2 cup roasted, unsalted peanuts
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
  - 2 tbsp lemon juice
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  - Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions:

1.  **Roast the Capsicums:** Preheat your oven grill (broiler) to high. Place the capsicums on a baking sheet and grill for 10-15 minutes, turning occasionally, until the skin is blackened and blistered all over.
2.  **Steam and Peel:** Transfer the hot capsicums to a bowl and cover tightly with plastic wrap. Let them steam for 10 minutes. This will make the skins easier to peel. Once cool enough to handle, peel away the skins, remove the stems and seeds.
3.  **Blend the Dip:** In a food processor, combine the roasted capsicums, peanuts, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, smoked paprika, and cumin.
4.  **Emulsify:** Blend until the mixture is smooth and creamy. If the dip is too thick, you can add a tablespoon of water or a little more olive oil to reach your desired consistency.
5.  **Season and Serve:** Season with salt and pepper to taste. Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Notes:

For a bit of heat, add a pinch of cayenne pepper or a small fresh chili to the food processor. The dip can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
