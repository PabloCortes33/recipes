# Simple Garlic Butter Pasta

A quick and easy pasta dish perfect for testing the recipe system. This classic combination of garlic, butter, and pasta creates a comforting meal in minutes.

**Yields:** 4 servings
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Cook time:** 15 minutes

**Ingredients:**
*   1 pound (454g) spaghetti or linguine
*   6 tablespoons unsalted butter
*   6 cloves garlic, minced
*   1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
*   1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
*   1/2 cup reserved pasta water
*   1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
*   Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
*   Zest of 1 lemon (optional)

**Equipment:**
*   Large pot for boiling pasta
*   Large skillet or sauté pan
*   Grater for Parmesan
*   Tongs or pasta fork

**Instructions:**

1.  **Cook the Pasta:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente, usually 8-10 minutes. Before draining, reserve 1/2 cup of the pasta cooking water. Drain the pasta and set aside.
2.  **Make the Garlic Butter Sauce:** While the pasta is cooking, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring frequently, until the garlic is fragrant and just starting to turn golden, about 2-3 minutes. Be careful not to burn the garlic.
3.  **Combine:** Add the cooked pasta to the skillet with the garlic butter. Toss to coat, adding reserved pasta water a little at a time until the sauce coats the pasta nicely and isn't dry.
4.  **Finish and Serve:** Remove from heat and stir in the chopped parsley and grated Parmesan cheese. Season with salt and pepper to taste. If using, add lemon zest for brightness. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan on the side.

**Notes:**
*   For extra richness, add a splash of heavy cream to the sauce
*   This recipe is easily customizable - add sautéed mushrooms, cherry tomatoes, or grilled chicken
*   The pasta water is key - its starch helps create a silky sauce that clings to the pasta
