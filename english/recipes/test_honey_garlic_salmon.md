# Honey Garlic Salmon

## Description
A quick and flavorful salmon dish with a sweet and savory glaze, perfect for weeknight dinners.

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds (optional)
- 2 green onions, sliced (for garnish)
- Lemon wedges (for serving)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the marinade**: In a small bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, and grated ginger until well combined.

2. **Season the salmon**: Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt and pepper.

3. **Sear the salmon**: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place salmon fillets skin-side up and cook for 3-4 minutes until golden brown.

4. **Flip and glaze**: Carefully flip the fillets. Pour the honey garlic sauce over the salmon and reduce heat to medium. Cook for another 3-4 minutes, spooning the sauce over the fish occasionally.

5. **Check doneness**: The salmon is ready when it flakes easily with a fork and reaches an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).

6. **Garnish and serve**: Transfer to plates, drizzle with remaining sauce from the pan, and garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Cooking Time
- Prep time: 10 minutes
- Cook time: 8 minutes
- Total time: 18 minutes

## Servings
4 servings
